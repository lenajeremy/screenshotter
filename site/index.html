How it works

One screenshot in. Finished designs out.

Drop in a screen from your app. Screenshotter frames it in the right device, builds a backdrop from your app's own colours and sets the type. Pick the one you like, then change anything.

  • Real captures from the iOS Simulator, a website or your Mac
  • Every App Store size for iPhone, iPad, Watch and Mac
  • Images and video, ready to upload

Version 0.3.0 · What's new · 30 MB · macOS 14 or later · Apple silicon · Needs Node.js 20+

  1. 01Install

    Open the download and drag Screenshotter into Applications.

  2. 02First launch

    If macOS says it can't check the app, open System Settings › Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway.

  3. 03Node.js

    The app uses the Node.js on your Mac. If you don't have it: brew install node.

# or build it yourself, from the repository
npm install && npm run app:build && open build/Screenshotter.app
